LOKESH THAKUR — AUTHOR, STORYTELLER, AND PHILOSOPHICAL THINKER — CHRONICLING HUMAN TRUTHS, EMPATHY, PARENTING, AND THE SUBTLE NUANCES OF INTERPERSONAL RELATIONSHIPS. AUTHOR OF THE ACCLAIMED WORKS: "CO-MAT-OSE" (कहानी-संग्रह) — AN EVOCATIVE ANTHOLOGY EXPLORING SURREAL EMOTIONAL THRESHOLDS, SILENT STRUGGLES, AND INNER AWAKENINGS; "DON'T BE A PARENT, BE A FRIEND" — A TRANSFORMATIVE PARADIGM FOR CONTEMPORARY MINDFUL PARENTING GROUNDED IN OPEN DIALOGUE, EMOTIONAL SAFETY, MUTUAL RESPECT, AND UNCONDITIONAL COMPANIONSHIP OVER RIGID AUTHORITY; "TWENTY20 OF LIFE" — 20 STRATEGIC, REFLECTIVE LESSONS FROM THE DYNAMICS OF MODERN CRICKET APPLIED TO RESILIENCE, MINDFUL LIVING, PATIENCE, LEADERSHIP, AND INNER PEACE. STORIES — PERSPECTIVES — REFLECTIONS — CONSCIOUS LIVING — CONTEMPORARY INDIAN LITERATURE — "TO WRITE IS TO LISTEN TO THE UNSPOKEN WHISPERS OF THE HEART." "PARENTING BEGINS WHEN DEMANDS SURRENDER TO UNDERSTANDING." "EVERY LIFE EXPERIENCE HOLDS A LESSON WAITING FOR PATIENT AWARENESS." Profile Introduction

A Lifelong Passion for Words & Human Truths

Lokesh Thakur is an author, thinker, and educator whose work traverses the intersections of literary storytelling, modern parenting, and philosophical life reflections.

Rooted in close observation of relationships, his books examine interpersonal dynamics with empathy. Through accessible storytelling, Lokesh encourages readers to embrace patience, dialogue, and mindful choices.

Personal Journey

Crafting Perspectives from Real Experiences

Lokesh's writing stems from observing subtle shifts within families and communities. Grounded in real conversations, his work explores parenting vulnerabilities and the universal need for meaningful connection.

His work began with short stories capturing lives caught between tradition and modern aspirations, culminating in the evocative anthology Co-Mat-Ose.
