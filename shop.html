Life Lessons • Sports Philosophy Motivational Insights

Twenty20 of Life

Cricketing Life Lessons
Author: Lokesh Thakur

Cricket is far more than a sport in India—it is a crucible of temperament, calculated courage, team synergy, and resilience under pressure. In Twenty20 of Life, Lokesh Thakur extracts powerful metaphors from the twenty overs of a T20 match to offer actionable wisdom for daily life and professional challenges.

Whether recovering from a lost wicket, managing changing run-rates, or backing oneself in the final over, this book inspires readers to face life's unpredictable deliveries with poise and unshakeable resolve.
